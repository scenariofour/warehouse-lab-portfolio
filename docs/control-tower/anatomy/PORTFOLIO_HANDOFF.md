# Personal portfolio handoff

## Short description

**The Anatomy of a Decision** is an editorial Tableau visualization tracing 14 warehouse data-quality questions across 11 registered sources and five proposed decision categories. Sage Green evidence associations and orange decision links lead into an investigation panel controlled by `pIssue`, keeping competing explanations and the next evidence request beside the selected question. Its 47 curves represent registered associations, not causal findings; decision framing remains AI-assisted and pending human review.
