## Question and decision

How do positive gap and picking excess change under current, literal-size and customer-dedup interpretations? Adjacent measures make opposing discrepancy directions visible; a single net-total difference would hide them. The decision is whether source-definition clarification is required before an operational claim.

## Repository findings and error lessons

| Interpretation | Gap units | Excess units |
|---|---:|---:|
| CURRENT_INFERRED | 13003 | 0 |
| LITERAL_SIZE | 78681 | 65678 |
| CUSTOMER_DEDUP | 12985 | 12717 |

These are R2-verified CSV quantities; complete rendered-branch QA remains pending. Customer dedup changes demand to 215460 versus 228195 under current/literal, so branch rates need their own denominator. [Calculation reference](../CALCULATION_REFERENCE.md#selected-interpretation-calculations).

Diagnostic errors: leaving picking/customer totals on Measure Values can overwhelm the intended comparison; filtering to one interpretation hides sensitivity; averaging or summing branches treats alternative worlds as independent observations. No apparent improvement is evidence that dedup is correct.

*My learning summary:* I learned to compare interpretations explicitly and preserve both gap directions. A calculation can improve in one metric while becoming harder to justify semantically.
