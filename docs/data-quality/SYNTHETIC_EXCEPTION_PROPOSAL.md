# Future synthetic inventory exception library — design only

2026-10-18 · PROPOSED, NOT APPROVED. No fixture records, production-like data, simulation features or synthetic registry entries are generated in M1–M3. This proposal does not assert these mechanisms occurred in the published warehouse.

Purpose: test whether future detection logic distinguishes an established record inconsistency from an unresolved operational explanation. Keep a separately versioned library/output directory with `data_origin=SYNTHETIC_TEST`, isolated fixture identifiers and an explicit approved fixture specification. Never append it to the real-source registry, public measures, published-source totals or production stock. Fixtures test rules, not prevalence, calibrated operating behavior or expected financial benefit.

| Mechanism | Required input fixtures (future only) | Detection expectation / operational implication / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Physical count versus ledger discrepancy | Same SKU/location/lot/UOM, aligned as-of timestamp, independently specified ledger quantity and blind count, movements during counting, count-method metadata | Calculate signed count−ledger after approved cutoff treatment. Flag variance for recount/investigation; no automatic write-off or theft claim. Tolerance and materiality must be owner-defined, not a generic percentage. |
| Reservation or status makes stock unavailable | Physical on-hand, physical reservations, inventory dimensions, blocked/quarantine status, allocation requests and consistent effective times | Report physical availability separately from nominal stock. Test a reservation exceeding eligible available stock under the chosen contract; alert is not proof of loss. ERP negative-stock/backorder rules and reservation scope must be declared. |
| Transfer timing / one-sided movement | Matching transfer identity, from/to locations, issued/received/in-transit states, timestamps/time zones and snapshot cutoffs | Reconcile source decrease, in-transit state and destination receipt. Distinguish legitimate in-transit quantity from a missing event. Aging/escalation horizon is an explicit assumption needing operational review. |
| Duplicate event replay versus legitimate repeat | Stable event identity/idempotency definition, two delivery attempts, full payload, processing state; separate legitimate distinct event with equal business fields | Detect duplicate event identity according to the approved interface contract; preserve distinct events with identical quantities/attributes. Source tuple equality alone must not authorize deduplication. Event-ID semantics are a proposed test contract, not supplied by current warehouse data. |
| UOM/pack conversion mismatch | Authoritative SKU-size and UOM master, effective dates, conversion ratios, transaction quantity/UOM, case/each/lot dimensions | Compare only after approved dimension and UOM alignment; retain original quantity/UOM and conversion lineage. Missing/ambiguous conversion yields UNKNOWN rather than invented stock. Ratios and rounding are fixture assumptions requiring approval; current size-like tokens are not pack ratios. |
| Missing or inconsistent product/location identity | Effective-dated product/location masters, event identifiers, authoritative aliases and an intentionally absent/retired key condition | Detect null or unmatched keys; distinguish missing master scope from invalid physical objects. No invented coordinate, replacement SKU or reachability conclusion. Current warehouse identifiers must not be falsely called GS1-conformant IDs. |
| Snapshot cutoff / out-of-order events | Opening balance, timestamped receipt/issue/adjustment events, effective and recorded times, explicit cutoff, time zone and late-arrival rules | Check conservation over the declared interval and surface timing sensitivity. Opening + receipts − issues + signed adjustments should equal closing only within aligned scope/UOM and completeness assumptions. Never force equality by deleting late events. |

Industry support checked 2026-10-18:

- [Microsoft inventory on-hand](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/inventory/inventory-on-hand-list) distinguishes physical, physically reserved and available quantities; its physical-availability definition subtracts physical reservations. [Inventory statuses](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/inventory/inventory-statuses) documents blocked inventory. These support the availability/status distinction, not universal ERP validation rules or a finding about this warehouse.
- [Microsoft inventory journals](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/supply-chain/inventory/inventory-journals) documents counting, transfer and adjustment workflows. It motivates count/ledger reconciliation and movement evidence; acceptable variance and cutoff policies remain owner decisions.
- [GS1 traceability](https://www.gs1.org/standards/traceability) distinguishes critical tracking events and their key data elements. [Global Traceability Standard](https://www.gs1.org/standards/gs1-global-traceability-standard/current-standard) provides object, location, time/time-zone and business context. These inform fixture lineage and identity. They do not make repeated rows errors, prescribe fixture frequencies or authenticate current source IDs.

The conservation equation, repeat-event idempotency behavior, UOM conversion expectations, cutoff intervals, negative-stock treatment, aging and tolerances are declared candidate test contracts; no claim of universal industry thresholds or formal standards compliance is made. No probabilities, exception rates, labor times, demand distributions, carton dimensions or monetary effects are inferred from these sources.

Before implementation, the project owner separately approves scope and isolation; a data/domain reviewer supplies fixture semantics/UOM/time/status rules and pass/fail expectations, including normal cases, counterexamples, ambiguous cases and expected UNKNOWN outcomes. An independent reviewer checks contamination safeguards and whether detections overclaim certainty. Record fixture specification/version/hash, rule source, assumptions, expected outputs, actual test results and residual limits. Authorizing fixture work would not approve production corrections, new simulations or G0–G4.
